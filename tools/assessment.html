Display and feedback

Camera, microphone and online processing each ask for permission when first used. Stop and Privacy mode are always available.

Your answers are saved to your Astha account so they follow you to another phone. You can export or delete them in Settings.

Astha works with the phone camera alone. A paired Wi-Fi camera and a Braille controller are optional.

Clip-on or glasses camera

A clip-on camera or smart glasses connect over your own Wi-Fi with a private pairing code. Images go from the camera to this phone only.

Six-key Braille controller

The wireless controller is not built yet, so nothing can be paired. You can practise its key combinations now on a computer keyboard: F, D and S are dots 1, 2 and 3; J, K and L are dots 4, 5 and 6. Press the keys together and release.

Braille controller: not connected.

Controller commands

    Distance sensor

    An ultrasonic distance sensor is planned. None is connected, so Astha does not report measured distances. Distances it mentions are rough estimates from the image.

    Other apps and screen control

    This version can read a screen you explicitly share. Controlling other apps is not connected yet. Camera permission does not enable it.

    In the Android app you can let Astha read other apps aloud. When you ask, it reads the text on the screen and can go back or home. It does not watch in the background, never reads password fields, and cannot tap, type or send anything for you. Android shows its own warning before it is turned on, and you can turn it off here at any time.

    Reading other apps is off.

    Messages

    Reading and answering messages from apps such as Messenger works in the ASTHA Android app.

    Astha can tell you when a message arrives in an app such as Messenger or WhatsApp, read it to you, and send the reply you dictate or one she suggests. Nothing is sent until you say yes, and messages are never saved. Android asks you to allow notification access first.

    Message help is off.

    Astha keeps a notification showing while this is on, with a Turn off button. She listens only for a few seconds right after reading a message or asking a question, and waits while you are on a call.

    You can also say “open the WhatsApp app”, or “write a message saying I will be late”. Astha asks before opening anything, and you choose the person and press send yourself.
